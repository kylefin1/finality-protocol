or make disclosed verifier implementations disagree under the published
assumptions. Do not begin with a desired conclusion. Report successful and
failed attacks, exact methods, limitations, and all discrepancies.

after artifact verification. This is not a funded bug bounty and does not
promise compensation, employment, or endorsement.
