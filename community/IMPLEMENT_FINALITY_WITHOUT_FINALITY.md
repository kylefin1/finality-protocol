# Implement Finality without Finality

Build a compatible implementation of the frozen `FRP-2.0.0-draft.1` /
`FSK-1.0.0` public semantics using only the specification, schemas, and vectors.

Your implementation remains yours. Disclose reused code, keep discrepancies
visible, publish exact commits and artifact hashes, and sign the report with
your own identity. Finality may answer specification questions but may not
secretly patch an implementation presented as independent.

Attributable compatible implementations may receive public credit, permanent
record linkage, and first-compatible-implementation recognition after the
submitted artifacts are independently rebuilt and checked. No employment,
customer relationship, endorsement, or payment is implied.
