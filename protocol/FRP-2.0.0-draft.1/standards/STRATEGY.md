# Standards and conformance strategy

Current status: Finality Group draft only. No NIST, IETF, W3C, SCITT, industry
consortium, or other standards body has accepted or endorsed the protocol.

Seller-controlled preparation includes a normative specification, algorithm
agility, version negotiation, VC-compatible and SCITT-style profiles,
conformance vectors, and transparent limitations. Any submission sequence must
follow specialist IP/counsel review to choose open specification, defensive
publication, patent candidate, trade-secret boundary, reference license, and
conformance-mark controls. Adoption should remain possible without Finality
cloud and without patent-locking the semantic abstraction.
