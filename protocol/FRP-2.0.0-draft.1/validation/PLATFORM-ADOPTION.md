# Platform adoption and evidence program

Evidence stages are `P0 inspected`, `P1 verifier used`, `P2 contract authored`,
`P3 test integration`, `P4 third-party implementation`, `P5 production
issuance`, `P6 cross-organization exchange`, and `P7 platform dependency`.
Downloads and meetings do not advance a stage.

Each record requires institution, disclosure permission, date, workflow,
protocol/release, evidence artifact hash, independence status, and publication
permission. Metrics default to zero. The strongest near-term milestone is an
outside organization signing an EVR after attempting false FINAL under a frozen
scope.
